Pick a photo, then drag inside any frame (or on the full photo) to choose which part shows. Frames match the site.

The whole photo · click or drag to aim

How it works: each photo has one focus point, used in every frame it appears in. The frames only crop; they never zoom in more than needed to fill the box. The coloured outlines on the whole photo show what each frame keeps. Arrow keys nudge by 1% (hold Shift for 5%). Your choices are remembered in this browser, then press Copy my crops and paste the result back to Claude.
